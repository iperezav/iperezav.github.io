About

Applied mathematician and workflow architect focused on nonlinear control, optimization, and scientific software.

I am a business analyst and applied mathematician working at the intersection of nonlinear control, optimization, and computational tooling. I build scalable, reproducible pipelines that connect legacy Microsoft BI stacks with modern Python, Julia, and Rust ecosystems.

My work emphasizes:

  • • Chen–Fliess series and operator‑theoretic perspectives on dynamics.
  • • Cross‑language benchmarking and reproducible scientific workflows.

Research

Chen–Fliess series for nonlinear systems
Algebraic and computational frameworks for Chen–Fliess expansions and Lie‑algebraic structure.
I develop symbolic and numeric routines for Chen–Fliess series, enabling systematic computation of iterated integrals, Lie brackets, and higher‑order approximations for control‑affine systems.
